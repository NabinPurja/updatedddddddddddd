Follow Us

At Urban Sprout, we believe there’s something special about caring for a garden — watching things grow, getting your hands in the soil, and creating a space you can be proud of. We’re here to make that experience easier and more enjoyable with gardening tools you can rely on. Whether you’re planting your first flowers or maintaining a garden you’ve loved for years, our tools are made to feel comfortable, last long, and help you get the job done right. For us, it’s not just about selling tools — it’s about supporting the joy of gardening and helping every customer bring their outdoor space to life.
